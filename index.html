PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026. Nippet (“we”, “us”) is a clipboard and keyboard utility by Nomli Tech Limited.

1. Data we process

  • Clipboard snippets, labels, folders, and fill-in templates you save.
  • Subscription status from Apple, used only to unlock Pro.

2. How data is stored

  • Snippets are stored locally on your device.
  • The keyboard reads that same on-device store through Apple App Groups.
  • If iCloud Backup or device backup is on, Apple may include app data under your Apple account settings.

3. Data sharing

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send snippets to our servers. There is no Nippet backend for clip content.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only what StoreKit needs to know if you are Pro.

4. Keyboard access

  • Save and paste from the keyboard need Allow Full Access in iOS Settings.
  • Clipboard is read only to save a snippet or resolve a fill-in such as Last copy.
  • You can type on Nippet without switching back to ABC. The system still uses Apple’s keyboard for passwords and phone pads.

5. Your choices

  • Delete snippets in the app at any time, including Clear All Clips.
  • Remove the Nippet keyboard in Settings → General → Keyboard.
  • Cancel Pro in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

6. Children

Nippet is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data, email us to request deletion.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page.
