Privacy Policy

Nippet ("we", "us") is a clipboard and keyboard utility app. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1) Data we process

  • Clipboard snippets and folders you save in the app.
  • Subscription status needed to unlock Pro features.

2) How data is stored

  • Snippets are stored locally on your device.
  • Keyboard/app shared state uses Apple App Groups on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud backup/device backup, Apple services may back up app data under your Apple account settings.

3) Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run an external data broker service for your snippets.
  • For purchases, Apple and RevenueCat process transaction metadata needed to validate and manage subscriptions.

4) Keyboard permission notice

  • The Nippet keyboard requires "Allow Full Access" for clipboard save/paste features.
  • Clipboard reads/writes are used only to provide keyboard functionality.

5) Your choices

  • You can delete snippets inside the app at any time.
  • You can disable keyboard access in iOS Settings.
  • You can cancel subscriptions in Apple Subscriptions settings.

6) Children's privacy

Nippet is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us to request deletion.

7) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page.
